Place official reference PDFs in this folder. They are automatically
loaded, chunked, embedded, and indexed into FAISS when the app starts.

Suggested documents to add (download official PDFs from NEPRA / your DISCO
/ government sources — do not use unofficial or unverified copies):

1. NEPRA current consumer-end tariff schedules (by DISCO / category)
2. Official tariff determination notifications
3. Fuel Charges Adjustment (FCA) monthly decisions/notifications
4. Quarterly Tariff Adjustment (QTA) decisions
5. Consumer bill-format explanation guides (if published by NEPRA/DISCOs)
6. Official electricity consumer complaint handling procedures
7. Consumer protection / rights information published by NEPRA

The app will run without any documents here, but charge explanations will
rely more heavily on "Requires Verification" labels since no official
source can be cited.

Do not commit copyrighted material you do not have rights to redistribute
in your own GitHub repo if it will be public — link to it instead where
appropriate, or use official freely published government PDFs.
